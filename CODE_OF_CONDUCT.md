# Code of Conduct

NoZZoMe is committed to a respectful, inclusive and technically constructive project
environment.

Participants must communicate professionally, welcome good-faith questions, respect
differing levels of experience, and criticize ideas or evidence rather than people.
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation and deliberate disruption
are not acceptable.

Potential violations should be reported privately to a Porto Space Team organization
owner rather than discussed publicly. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate
content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, code review and
other project-related communication.
