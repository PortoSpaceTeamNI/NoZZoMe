# Governance

NoZZoMe is maintained as an open research-software project associated with the Porto
Space Team.

## Roles

- Maintainers review contributions, manage releases and protect the consistency of
  the physical and numerical models.
- Contributors may propose code, documentation, tests, validation cases and research
  integrations through issues and pull requests.
- Scientific contributors are credited according to the substance of their work;
  software contribution does not automatically imply authorship of every paper.

## Decisions

Routine changes are decided through public pull-request review. Changes to governing
equations, validation thresholds, public APIs or file formats must document the
engineering rationale, tests and compatibility consequences. When consensus is not
reached, the lead maintainer makes the decision and records the reasoning publicly.

## Releases

Maintainers create tagged releases after the test suite and continuous-integration
workflow pass. Each release must update the changelog and citation metadata. Archived
research releases should be deposited in Zenodo and linked to the corresponding tag.

## Project continuity

Additional maintainers may be appointed after sustained, constructive contributions.
If the current lead maintainer becomes unavailable, stewardship may be transferred to
another active maintainer or to the Porto Space Team organization.
